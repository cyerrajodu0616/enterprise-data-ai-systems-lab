Business invariant

One Authoritative Current State

The 2 TB customer_current table receives 25M daily CDC rows. The result is the latest committed PostgreSQL state per customer_id. source_sequence is authoritative; application and ingestion timestamps are not source order.

Source contract→Deduplicate batch→Target guard→Policy-valid transition
Two correctness boundaries

Deterministic source cardinality prevents multiple batch rows matching one target. source.source_sequence > target.last_source_sequence prevents stale or duplicate records from overwriting newer state. Equal sequence with different payload is quarantined.

Sequence and policy

Sequence determines which event is newer. Business policy determines whether the transition is valid. Track last seen, applied, and rejected separately.

Event dependency

A complete after-image may be independent. A partial update may depend on rejected history; isolate that customer, reconcile, and replay in order.

Write amplification and deletion vectors

Lesson assumptions: 250k × 1 KB ≈ 250 MB logical; 2,400 × 128 MB ≈ 307 GB physical; about 1,228×. Without deletion vectors, a row change can rewrite its containing Parquet file. Enabled deletion vectors can defer immediate full-file rewrites through metadata-backed soft deletes, but upgrade the table protocol and require compatible clients. They are not automatic on every Delta table. These are documented platform facts, not repository measurements.

No-op trade-off

24.75M supplied events have unchanged attributes but newer sequence. Filtering saves rewrites but can lose an ordering boundary; preserve Bronze and validate per-key ordering/checkpoints or separate sequence state.

Atomicity and SLAs

Evaluate Row-Level Concurrency, Then Coordinate

The same-file conflict scenario represents behavior without applicable row-level concurrency. Current Databricks row-level concurrency can reduce different-row/same-file conflicts when requirements are met, including Runtime 14.3 LTS+, an unpartitioned table, and deletion vectors enabled. Conflicts that remain fail atomically and require the complete deterministic MERGE to retry.

Conflict evidence

The lesson reports 47 of 52 conflicts during backfill and 25-minute CDC retries. First validate current concurrency and residual conflicts. Use workload coordination as fallback/control when needed to protect CDC's 15-minute SLA while distinguishing physical file contention from key conflict.

Safe chunk window

15-minute interval − 6-minute p95 − 2-minute buffer ≈ 7 minutes. Use deterministic checkpointed chunks, never unordered LIMIT, and validate both SLAs.

The reported 18-hour backfill and 95% conflict decrease are lesson evidence, not LAB-015 measurements.

Industry and vendor reference layer

Our Design Versus Current Managed Platforms

Documented fact: Databricks AUTO CDC uses KEYS, SEQUENCE BY, and APPLY AS DELETE WHEN for managed SCD1/SCD2 processing, including out-of-order handling by sequence and temporary managed SCD2 tombstones. Architectural inference: prefer it when those semantics express the contract; keep manual MERGE for justified exceptional policy.

Snowflake Streams expose METADATA$ACTION, METADATA$ISUPDATE, and METADATA$ROW_ID; updates appear as DELETE/INSERT pairs. Streams + Tasks + MERGE suit procedural DML/control, while Dynamic Tables simplify supported declarative SELECT pipelines. A Stream offset is not the original external source's business order.

Representative industry practices

Netflix DBLog: watermark-coordinated snapshot and log CDC with resumable chunks. LinkedIn Brooklin: bootstrap, transaction boundaries, and operational-store isolation. Uber DBEvents: bootstrap plus incremental, standardized events. Airbnb SpinalTap: reliable low-latency standardized CDC events. The sources do not establish use of this lesson's exact tombstone schema.

Start with the correctness contract: authoritative key, authoritative ordering, delete semantics, replay horizon, and reactivation policy. On Databricks, prefer AUTO CDC when it expresses those requirements; then evaluate deletion vectors, liquid clustering, and row-level concurrency before creating custom workload coordination. On Snowflake, choose between Streams + Tasks + MERGE and a declarative Dynamic Table based on required DML and control. Custom logic must earn its operational cost.

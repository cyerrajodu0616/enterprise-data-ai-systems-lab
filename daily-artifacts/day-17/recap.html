Business invariant

One Authoritative Current State

The 2 TB customer_current table receives 25M daily CDC rows. The result is the latest committed PostgreSQL state per customer_id. source_sequence is authoritative; application and ingestion timestamps are not source order.

Source contract→Deduplicate batch→Target guard→Policy-valid transition
Two correctness boundaries

Deterministic source cardinality prevents multiple batch rows matching one target. source.source_sequence > target.last_source_sequence prevents stale or duplicate records from overwriting newer state. Equal sequence with different payload is quarantined.

Sequence and policy

Sequence determines which event is newer. Business policy determines whether the transition is valid. Track last seen, applied, and rejected separately.

Event dependency

A complete after-image may be independent. A partial update may depend on rejected history; isolate that customer, reconcile, and replay in order.

Write amplification

Lesson assumptions: 250k × 1 KB ≈ 250 MB logical; 2,400 × 128 MB ≈ 307 GB physical; about 1,228×. These are not repository measurements.

No-op trade-off

24.75M supplied events have unchanged attributes but newer sequence. Filtering saves rewrites but can lose an ordering boundary; preserve Bronze and validate per-key ordering/checkpoints or separate sequence state.

Atomicity and SLAs

Retry the Transaction, Coordinate the Writers

In conventional copy-on-write, disjoint keys in one file may contend. A stale transaction fails atomically; rerun the complete deterministic MERGE. Actual behavior depends on runtime, isolation, table features, predicates, and row-level concurrency.

Conflict evidence

The lesson reports 47 of 52 conflicts during backfill and 25-minute CDC retries. Prioritize CDC's 15-minute SLA over resumable 24-hour backfill, distinguishing physical file contention from key conflict.

Safe chunk window

15-minute interval − 6-minute p95 − 2-minute buffer ≈ 7 minutes. Use deterministic checkpointed chunks, never unordered LIMIT, and validate both SLAs.

The reported 18-hour backfill and 95% conflict decrease are lesson evidence, not LAB-015 measurements.
